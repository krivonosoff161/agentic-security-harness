## Research archive

Files not listed above are retained because many are exact contract descriptions,
experiment status records, evidence projections, or bounded research notes. Their presence
does not mean every contour is a shipped product capability. Use
[Current state](current-state.md) and the [Capability matrix](capability-matrix.md) as the
authoritative navigation layer before relying on a specialized page.

Private prompts, raw model conversations, credentials, runtime databases, and private
custody evidence are intentionally not public documentation artifacts.
