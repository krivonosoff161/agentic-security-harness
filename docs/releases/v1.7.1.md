Status: **published public research release** on 2026-09-28. The v1.7.1
wheel and sdist are available on [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/agentic-security-harness/1.7.1/).

The scoped repairs are merged through
[PR #313](https://github.com/krivonosoff161/agentic-security-harness/pull/313).
Release metadata was merged through
[PR #319](https://github.com/krivonosoff161/agentic-security-harness/pull/319).
The five deeper foundation research questions remain open in issues #314–318;
they are not additional features of this patch.

## Patch scope

- Reject a nonempty child authority scope when the supplied parent scope is
  known empty; keep an absent parent scope distinct from a known empty scope.
- Narrow memory TTL against the original write epoch and the minimum finite
  write, stored and read TTL, so a later read cannot renew an expired entry.
- Add focused positive and negative regressions for both contract repairs.

The public foundation matrix maps broader research questions and residuals; it
does not turn those questions into completed security proofs or new 1.7.1
package claims. Historical v1.7.0 artifacts and observations remain unchanged.
